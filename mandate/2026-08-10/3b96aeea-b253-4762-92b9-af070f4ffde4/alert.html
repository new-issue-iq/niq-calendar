Deal summary
Martin Marietta Materials, Inc. (MLM) is arranging a series of fixed income investor calls on August 10th ahead of a potential benchmark senior unsecured fixed-rate USD SEC registered offering with maturities ranging from 3 to 30 years. The deal carries expected ratings of Baa2 (Negative Watch) / BBB+ (Negative) / BBB+ (Negative) from Moody's, S&P, and Fitch respectively, reflecting investment grade status with negative outlooks across all three agencies. Goldman Sachs & Co. LLC, J.P. Morgan, Deutsche Bank Securities, and Truist Securities, Inc. are serving as joint bookrunners, with Goldman Sachs coordinating logistics.
