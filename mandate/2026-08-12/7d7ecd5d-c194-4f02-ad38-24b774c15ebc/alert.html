The Company is supported by over 2,000 employees and a robust and substantial land bank and global development pipeline, positioning it among the largest data center platforms in North America. Through its technology-enabled platform, delivered across mega-scale data center infrastructure, the Company offers a comprehensive portfolio of secure and compliant information technology solutions.
