Deal summary
Beacon Point DC LLC, an indirect subsidiary of Hut 8 Corp., has mandated J.P. Morgan and Goldman Sachs & Co. LLC to arrange a series of fixed income investor calls ahead of a potential Rule 144A/Reg S offering, with ratings to follow from Moody's. The issuer is developing a 352 MW hyperscale data center in Nueces County, Texas, 100% leased to a high-investment-grade tenant (rated AA- or higher) under a 15-year triple-net lease structure with three 5-year extension options, providing strong credit support. The deal is at the Mandate stage with no size, tenor, or pricing terms disclosed; investor calls are scheduled across June 2nd and 3rd, 2026, accompanied by a draft preliminary offering memorandum and pre-recorded investor presentation.
