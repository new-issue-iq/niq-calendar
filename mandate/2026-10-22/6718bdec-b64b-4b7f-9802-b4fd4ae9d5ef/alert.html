Deal summary
Gulf Keystone Petroleum Ltd (GKP), a London Stock Exchange and Euronext Growth Oslo-listed oil producer operating the Shaikan Field in the Kurdistan Region of Iraq, is launching a USD-denominated senior unsecured bond with an initial issue amount of USD 175 million and a maximum issue amount of USD 300 million (plus any Additional Bonds from a ShaMaran Rollover), with a 5-year tenor and bullet maturity. The bond is structured to become senior secured from the Security Take-Up Date, pari passu with the ShaMaran Bond, and features a make-whole call for the first 30 months followed by a declining premium call schedule, with proceeds earmarked for near-term Shaikan Field capex and general corporate purposes. The deal is at the Mandate stage, with investor roadshow calls scheduled for October 23 and 26, 2026, and 1-on-1 meetings available from October 22, 2026; the coupon rate remains to be determined.
