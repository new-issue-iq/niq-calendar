Deal summary
Axis Bank is conducting investor calls for a potential US dollar-denominated dual-tranche offering comprising a Perpetual Non-Call 5.5-year Additional Tier 1 (AT1) subordinated note and/or a 5-year Senior note. The deal is at the mandate/investor call stage, with calls scheduled for Monday, 22 June 2026 across four small group slots in Hong Kong and UK time zones, organized by logistics banks HSBC (Asia) and JPM (EMEA). No pricing, size, or spread guidance has been provided at this stage; the issuer will be represented by CFO Puneet Sharma and Group Head of Markets, ALM & Syndication Rituparna Banerjee.
