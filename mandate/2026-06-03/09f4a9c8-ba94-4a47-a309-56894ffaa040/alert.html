Deal summary
QBE Insurance Group Limited (QBEAU), rated A (Stable) by both S&P and Fitch at the issuer level, has mandated Barclays, Crédit Agricole CIB, Deutsche Bank, J.P. Morgan, and Natixis as Joint Lead Managers for a EUR 300m–500m Regulation S Registered 11NC6 Tier 2 Subordinated Notes transaction, with expected securities ratings of BBB+ from both S&P and Fitch. The deal is at the Mandate stage, with a series of fixed-income investor meetings commencing Thursday 4 June 2026, and a potential transaction to follow subject to market conditions. The 11NC6 structure implies an 11-year maturity with a 6-year non-call period, consistent with Tier 2 insurance capital instruments.
