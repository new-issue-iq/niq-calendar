Deal summary
Tecpetrol S.A., Argentina's leading shale gas operator, has mandated BBVA, Itaú BBA, J.P. Morgan, Santander (Global Coordinators and Joint Bookrunners), and Balanz (Joint Bookrunner) to arrange fixed-income investor meetings ahead of a potential benchmark-sized reopening of its existing USD-denominated 144A/Reg S 7.625% Senior Unsecured Notes due 2033. The transaction carries expected ratings of Ba3/BB- (Moody's/Fitch), firmly placing it in high-yield territory, and is subject to market conditions following the investor engagement process beginning September 3, 2026.
